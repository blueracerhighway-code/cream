# brag plan — The Sunnah Foods (thesunnahfoods.ca)

**What it is:** Raw Alberta honey, creamed for 2 weeks and infused with black seed, sesame seed and cold-pressed olive oil. It's black seed without the bitter taste.
**Who it's for:** people who've tried black seed for its benefits and quit because of the taste.
**What sets it apart:** it's "bitterless". Customers say it's "wayy better" than black seed oil.
**Visual hook:** the dark, glossy honey swirl.
**Real UI shown:** the hero, the badge marquee, the review carousel, the "Clinically Proven Results" stats, the Honey Profile, and the "Choose Your Offer" bundle picker with its add-to-cart button.
**Tone:** `default`, leaning warm and polished: soft, honeyed and punchy.
**Format:** 1080×1920, 30fps, 20.0s.

**Angle:** a scroll down the page, from "Bitterless" to "Add to cart". Each section of the site gets one beat, and the cuts land on the bar lines of a 96 BPM track (one bar = 2.5s).

**Visual identity (taken from the site's CSS):** cream `#F9F0E7` / `#FFF4E0`, honey `#F0B860`, amber `#E0A24C`, brown `#3D2A18`, ink `#1A1A1A`, gold `#9A6B1E`. Fonts: Fraunces for display type, Manrope for body text, DM Sans for the offer UI.
