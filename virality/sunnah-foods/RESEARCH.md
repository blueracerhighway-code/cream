Collected 2026-10-05. YouTube Shorts are public data pulled with yt-dlp, with no login and no cookies. Each video is ranked
as a multiple of its own channel's median views, using `swipe.py`.

## What the winners share

1. **They open on a problem the viewer already has with black seed** (why take it daily, why your routine is failing).
   They don't open on the product. This lines up exactly with the "compliance gap" in Part 7 of the source doc. Used in
   script 05.
2. **Family or heritage approval** ("my grandma swears by this") and **gifting moments** (Ramadan gift). Used in
   scripts 06 and 08.
3. **Close-up texture and nature footage** (honeycomb, blossoms, creamed versus liquid). Every script asks for close-up
   spoon pulls and texture B-roll.

On the founder-journey side, the best-documented pattern is the unscripted founder origin story. Poppi's co-founder posted
one and, by the brand's account, it brought in about $100K in sales within 24 hours (NoGood). Your own source doc already
picks this as your edge: honest money numbers and failures. Eight of the ten scripts lead with one of your real numbers.

## Limits, said plainly

- **TikTok and Instagram weren't scraped.** Those numbers sit behind a login. If you paste 20 to 30 posts from creators
  you watch, in this format: `@creator | views | first line | url`, I'll re-rank them.
- **Transcripts weren't available.** YouTube now asks for a login to download captions, so the breakdown above comes from
  titles, view counts and multiples, not the spoken words.
- **Your own past posts are the strongest evidence**, and we don't have them yet. Paste your last 20 videos with view
  counts and the next batch gets built from your own winners.
- A hook-formula label describes the words. It doesn't explain why a video got its views. Nothing here guarantees reach.
